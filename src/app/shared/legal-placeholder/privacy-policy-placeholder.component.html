1. INTRODUCTION

Welcome to NotaGo, a Digital Trust, Verification and Notarization Platform owned and operated by Afrilight Technologies Ltd. ("Afrilight", "NotaGo", "we", "our", or "us").

NotaGo enables individuals, businesses, government agencies and other organizations to create, verify, electronically sign, notarize and authenticate documents through a secure online platform.

Protecting your privacy and personal information is fundamental to the trust placed in our platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose and protect your personal information whenever you access or use the NotaGo website, mobile applications, APIs and related services (collectively referred to as the "Platform").

By creating an account, accessing or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and consent to the processing of your personal information as described herein.

2. OUR COMMITMENT TO PRIVACY

Privacy is one of the core pillars upon which NotaGo is built.

Our objective is to provide a secure digital environment where users can confidently complete legally significant transactions while maintaining the confidentiality, integrity and availability of their personal information.

Every identity verification, document signing, notarization session and verification request is processed using secure technologies designed to maintain the integrity and authenticity of every transaction.

  • Bank Verification Number (BVN)

For Organizations:

Where users generate documents using NotaGo templates, we collect the information entered into questionnaires.

  • Reason for Change

  • Marriage Details
  • Residential Address

When a document is electronically signed, we collect information relating to the signing event, including:

  • Device Information
  • Browser Information
  • Geographic Region (where available)

During an online notarization session, we may collect:

  • Session End Time
  • Assigned Notary
  • Identity Confirmation Results
  • Oath Administration Confirmation
  • Session Outcome

Where permitted by law or where necessary to preserve evidence of the notarization process, certain session information may be retained as part of the transaction audit record.

10.7 Payment Information

Payments made through NotaGo are processed by approved third-party payment providers.

Depending on the payment method selected, we may receive:
