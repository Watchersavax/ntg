Agents / Freelancers

Profile

Agents or Professionals Assisting with Digital Platforms account is specifically designed for those who help individuals who are not well-versed with digital technology or lack direct access to it. These professionals play a crucial role in bridging the digital divide by facilitating the use of the e-Affidavit platform, making it accessible to a wider audience.

This account is essential for ensuring that all community members, regardless of their digital skills or technology access, can benefit from the services provided by the e-Affidavit platform.

Verification

To maintain the integrity and security of the e-Affidavit platform, Agents must undergo a thorough verification process. They are verified through the Lagos State Residents Registration Agency (LASRRA) to confirm their identity and legitimacy.

Additionally, they require mandatory approval from the Ministry of Justice (MOJ) before their accounts can be activated.

This two-step verification process ensures that only qualified and authorized individuals can offer these services, upholding the platform's legal and ethical standards.

Account Usage

Once verified and approved, professionals can create and manage affidavits on behalf of their clients. They are responsible for ensuring the accuracy of the information entered and for complying with all procedural guidelines of the e-Affidavit platform.

Through their personalized accounts, they can easily submit requests, track progress, and handle documentation efficiently.

To ensure their success, the platform offers comprehensive training resources and dedicated support from the administrative team. This empowers agents and freelancers to navigate the platform with confidence and address any user concerns that may arise.

Additionally, the e-Affidavit platform employs robust monitoring tools to oversee the activities of agents and freelancers. This safeguards the integrity of the system and helps maintain compliance with operational protocols, preventing any misuse.
