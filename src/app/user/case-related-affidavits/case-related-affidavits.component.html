Case-Related Affidavits

Please follow these steps to submit your case-related affidavit for the Commissioner of Oath's signature:

  • Upload Affidavit: Upload your case-related affidavit in PDF format. Please ensure the file size does not exceed 5MB.
  • Sign Document: Prior to uploading, ensure that you have signed the affidavit.
  • Make Payment: Complete the required payment for the affidavit processing.
  • Schedule Video Conference: Schedule a video conference appointment with the Commissioner of Oaths.
  • Commissioner's Signature: The Commissioner of Oaths will review and sign your affidavit during the video conference.
  • Receive Signed Affidavit: Once signed, the affidavit will be sent to your email address and a copy will be saved on your profile page within the e-affidavit platform for future reference.
