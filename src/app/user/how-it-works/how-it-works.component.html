How it Works

1

Account Registration (Sign Up)

To get started, create your account on the e-Affidavit platform by entering your personal information and setting a secure password.

2

Select an Affidavit

Choose the appropriate affidavit type from a categorized list available on the platform or upload a pre-prepared affidavit document.

3

Answer the Questionnaire

Fill out a detailed questionnaire to automatically populate the affidavit with the necessary information.

4

Pay for Affidavit

Complete the payment for the affidavit processing using the platform's secure online payment system.

5

Provide Your Government-Issued ID Number

Enter your National Identification Number (NIN), Driver’s License, LASRRA ID or International Passport for identity verification.

6

Sign the Affidavit

Electronically sign your affidavit using a secure digital signature feature provided on the platform.

7

Schedule a Video Conference with the Commissioner of Oaths

Arrange a video call through the platform to verify your identity and affidavit details with the Commissioner of Oaths.

8

Attend the Meeting

Participate in the scheduled video conference with the Commissioner of Oaths to complete the verification process.

9

Affidavit Signed by Commissioner of Oaths

The Commissioner of Oaths officially signs your affidavit during the video call, finalizing the document.

10

Download the Signed Affidavit from Your Account, a Copy is in Your Email too

Access and download your legally signed affidavit from your account. A copy is also sent to your registered email for your records.
